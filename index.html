Johnson & Johnson distribution case study

May 2026

Analyzed 450+ lines of 2024 order data (119,000+ units) to support shipper supply planning.

  • Built PivotTables comparing monthly volume by shipper type (RFG vs. CRT) and size.
  • Found that RFG shippers made up about 88% of total volume, so inventory planning should prioritize RFG supply.
  • Flagged demand spikes, including RFG Small in February and December and RFG Large in July, and recommended planning buffers for them.
  • Excel PivotTables
  • Demand forecasting
